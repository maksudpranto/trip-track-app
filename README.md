## ✨ Features

- **Smart Expense Splitting & Debt Minimization:** Log shared expenses, specify who paid, and let TripTrack calculate the optimal, fewest number of payments required to settle all debts.
- **Exportable PDF Settlements:** Generate polished, transparent PDF balance sheets to share directly with your tour mates.
- **Offline-First Architecture:** Track expenses and view your schedule anywhere — even in remote areas with zero cell signal. Everything synchronizes automatically once connectivity is restored.
- **Interactive Day-by-Day Itineraries:** Keep daily timelines, activity categories, locations, times, and notes organized alongside your travel budget.
- **Multi-Currency Support:** Automatic currency symbol mapping and calculations for international adventures.
- **Dynamic Tour Lifecycle:** Smart transitions across Planning, Upcoming (with dynamic countdowns), Active, and Completed trips.
